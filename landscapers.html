Landscaper questions

Good questions from landscapers.

More answers on the FAQ page. Last updated December 2026.

What is the best website builder for landscapers?

It depends on how much you want to do yourself and what you mostly sell. Do-it-yourself builders like Wix or Squarespace cost less but leave design, writing and follow-up to you. Job software like Jobber includes a basic site that suits maintenance bookings. Agencies do it all but often charge thousands a month. ScrollBuilt gives landscapers a finished site in about 30 seconds, built to sell design-build projects, with lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests, from $249 a month billed yearly.

How much does a landscaping website cost?

Marketing agencies often charge $3,500 to $8,000 a month plus $12,000 to $25,000 to build. Do-it-yourself builders run about $16 to $159 a month, plus your time. ScrollBuilt is $249 a month billed yearly, or $299 month to month, with no setup fee. Your draft is free and you only pay when your site goes live.

How fast can I get a website for my landscaping company?

Your draft takes about 30 seconds: type your business name and city and it appears with a landscape build film and your name on it. Going live is as fast as you add your photos and connect your web address. We can launch it for you for a one-time $499.

I already use Jobber. Do I need a separate website?

Not necessarily. Jobber's included site suits maintenance bookings. If you sell design-build projects, a site built to make homeowners picture the finished yard usually wins more of them. Many landscapers keep Jobber for scheduling and invoicing and use ScrollBuilt for the website and lead follow-up.

Do I need to know how to build a website?

No. The pages, design and build film already exist. You change things by asking in plain words and approving a preview, and one change a month made by a person is included.

Will my landscaping company show up in ChatGPT or Google’s AI answers?

Nobody can promise an AI will recommend a business, and we won’t. Your site is set up so search engines and AI assistants can find it, read it and understand what you build and where. A monthly report shows how you show up.

Is the build film my own project?

No. It comes from a library of sample films ScrollBuilt makes for landscapers, matched to your part of the country: desert, wooded or coastal. Your own project photos go across the rest of the site, and you can switch films any time.

Can I take my website with me if I leave?

Your text, photos and leads are yours to download any time. On the annual plan, the full working site can go with you from day one. On the monthly plan, after three paid months, or earlier with a $999 buy-out.
