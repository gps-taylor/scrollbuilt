Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Wondour Inc., doing business as ScrollBuilt ("ScrollBuilt", "we"), handles personal information on scrollbuilt.com, in the ScrollBuilt app, and on the websites we host for our customers.

Two roles

  • For our own customers and prospects, we decide how your information is used. This covers people who sign up, use the app, make a draft site, fill in a form on scrollbuilt.com or contact us.
  • For visitors to our customers' websites, the business whose website you visit decides how your information is used, and we process it on their behalf. Examples include people who send a quote request, use the chat or book a consult on a contractor's site. Questions about that information should go to that business first, and we will help them respond.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell personal information. Site analytics do not use cookies. To limit spam and abuse, we store a salted hash of your IP address and not the address itself. Spam protection uses Cloudflare Turnstile.

Text messages

We send text messages only to people who have agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop and HELP for help. We do not share mobile numbers or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing. Details are at app.scrollbuilt.com/text-alerts.

Connected calendars

If you connect a Google or Outlook calendar, we read your events to show your schedule to you and to keep booked consults from clashing with it, and we add consults booked through your site to your calendar. We do not change or delete your other events, and we do not use calendar data for advertising or to train AI models. Access tokens are stored encrypted, and you can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes them. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How AI is used

Several features use AI models from our providers:

  • Building and editing a site. Site content and your request are sent to the provider to draft pages and suggest edits.
  • Website chat and replies to leads. A visitor's message, and the business's own site content, are sent to the provider to write an answer.
  • Questions you text or ask in the app. Your question and the relevant records from your account (for example, today's leads and bookings) are sent to the provider to write the answer.
  • Design visualizer. An uploaded photo is sent to the provider to create a concept image.

Our agreements with these providers do not allow them to use this information to train their models, and we do not use it to train models either.

How long we keep information

  • Account and site data: while the account is active, plus 90 days after a plan ends so a site can be downloaded or restored.
  • Draft sites nobody keeps: removed from view after 7 days.
  • Leads, chats and bookings: kept for the customer until they delete them or their account is deleted.
  • Billing records: as required by tax law.
  • Server logs: short-term only.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information by emailing support@scrollbuilt.com. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, and we will honor them as the law requires. If you sent a form, chat or booking on a customer's site, contact that business; we will help them with your request.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role, and each customer's data is kept separate. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will post updates here and, for material changes, notify customers by email.
