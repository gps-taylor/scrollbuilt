FAQ

Good questions.

Is this just a website?

No. You get the website plus the follow-up behind it: replies to new leads, website chat, consult booking, proposal pages and Google review requests. It all lives in one login.

What does “built for the AI era” mean?

More homeowners now ask AI who to hire. We set your site up automatically so AI can find it, read it and name your business. Website chat answers from your own site. A monthly report shows whether AI assistants mention you. Nobody can promise an AI will recommend a business, and we won’t.

What does it cost?

Your draft is free. To put it live yourself it is $149 a month, with no setup fee and no contract. To have us build and run it, it is $599 a month on a 12-month term, plus a one-time setup of $1,500 to $2,000 by the size of your site. After that term you can stay on, or host the site with us yourself for $99 a month.

How fast can I be live?

Your draft takes about 30 seconds, because the design, the pages and the build film for your trade already exist. From there it is as fast as you add your photos and choose a plan. On the done-for-you plan we do that with you after one 30-minute call, and you are live within two weeks.

What does “one contractor per territory” mean?

Your territory is up to five ZIP codes you choose, agreed in writing when you sign. For your first 12 months with us, we won’t build for another company in your trade in any of them. After that, your site, your price and everything else stay the same.

Do I have to move my domain?

No. Your domain stays registered where it is, in your name. We only change two settings so it points to your new site.

Can I leave?

You can download a working copy of your site, with its pages, photos and animation, and host it anywhere. Your domain, your content and your leads are yours.

Do I need professional photos?

No. Use what you have. On the done-for-you plan we make your build animation with AI, shaped around your real projects, and use your own project photos across the rest of the site.
