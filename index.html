FAQ

Good questions.

Is this just a website?

No. You get the website plus the front office behind it: replies to new leads, website chat, consult booking, proposal pages and Google review requests. It all lives in one login.

What does “built for the AI era” mean?

More homeowners now ask AI who to hire. We set your site up automatically so AI can find it, read it and name your business. Website chat answers from your own site. A monthly report shows whether AI assistants mention you. Nobody can promise an AI will recommend a business, and we won’t.

What does it cost?

$599 a month on a 12-month term, plus a one-time setup fee we quote on your call. After the term you can stay on, or move to self-managed hosting at $99 a month.

How can you launch in two weeks?

The system is already built for your kind of business. One 30-minute kickoff call gives us what we need. We write, design and build, you review once, and we launch on day 14.

What does “one contractor per territory” mean?

Your territory is the area you actually serve, set by ZIP codes and agreed in writing when you sign. Large metro areas have many territories; smaller ones may be just one. While you’re with us, we won’t build for another company in your trade there.

Do I have to move my domain?

No. Your domain stays registered where it is, in your name. We only change two settings so it points to your new site.

Can I leave?

You can download a working copy of your site, with its pages, photos and animation, and host it anywhere. Your domain, your content and your leads are yours.

Do I need professional photos?

No. Send what you have. We make your build animation with AI, shaped around your real projects, and use your own project photos across the rest of the site.
