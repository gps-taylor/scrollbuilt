FAQ

Good questions.

Is the animation just a video?

No. It’s a sequence of high-resolution frames tied to the visitor’s scroll, so they control the build. It loads progressively, works on phones and tablets, and has its own version made for vertical screens.

Do I need professional photos or renderings?

No. Send what you have. We generate your build sequence with AI, shaped around your real projects or a description of your signature job, and use your own project photos throughout the rest of the site.

How can you really launch in two weeks?

Because we’ve built the system for your kind of business. One 30-minute kickoff call gives us what we need; we write, design, generate and build, you review once, and we launch on day 14.

What does “one builder per market” mean?

At kickoff we agree on your market — usually your metro area — and your trade. While you’re with us, we won’t build for another company in that trade in that market. It’s in writing.

What if my market is already taken?

We’ll tell you right away. You’ll be first in line if that market opens up, and we can check neighboring markets or other trades you serve.

Will it help me show up on Google?

Every site is fast, mobile-first, and built with clean structure, local business markup and service-area pages. Nobody can honestly promise rankings, but you’ll start from a far stronger foundation than a template.

Do I own my domain and content?

Yes. Your domain, your photos, your reviews and your words stay yours.
