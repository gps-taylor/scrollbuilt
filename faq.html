FAQ

Good questions.

Money questions are answered in full on the pricing page.

Is this just a website?

No. You get the website plus the follow-up behind it: replies to new leads, website chat, a text back to missed callers, proposal pages and review requests. It all lives in one login.

What does it cost?

$249 a month, with no setup fee and no contract. Your draft is free and you pay when you put your site live. A few things are add-ons. See full pricing.

How fast can I be live?

Your draft takes about 30 seconds, because the design, the pages and the build film for your trade already exist. From there it is as fast as you add your photos and point your web address at it. If you would rather we did that, we launch it for you for a one-time $499.

Which trades is it for?

Pool builders and landscapers can see a draft right now. For custom homes, remodeling, roofing, ADUs and other trades that build, tell us what you do and we build your draft with you.

Is the build film my own project?

No. It comes from a library of films ScrollBuilt makes with AI for each trade and part of the country. Your own project photos go across the rest of the site. You can switch films any time.

Do I need professional photos?

No. The build film makes the site look finished before you add a single photo. Add your own project photos whenever you have them.

What does “built for the AI era” mean?

More homeowners now ask AI who to hire. We set your site up automatically so AI can find it, read it and name your business. Website chat answers from your own site. A monthly report shows whether AI assistants mention you. Nobody can promise an AI will recommend a business, and we won’t.

Do I have to move my domain?

No. Your domain stays registered where it is, in your name. Two settings are changed so it points to your new site. We can do that for you for a one-time $149.

Who makes changes to my site?

You can, by asking in plain words and approving the preview. One change a month made by a person is included. More than that is $49 a change, or $99 a month for unlimited small changes.

What is a protected territory?

Choose a 12-month term and we won’t build a site for another company in your trade located in your ZIP codes, up to five, for those 12 months. It is about where a company is located. It does not stop a company based somewhere else from working in your area. How it works.

Can I leave?

Yes, any time. One button downloads a working copy of your site, with its pages, photos, build film, redirects and leads, and you can host it anywhere. Your domain, your content and your leads are yours.

Still not sure? Ask us. We reply within one business day.
