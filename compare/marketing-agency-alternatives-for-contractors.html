Alternatives to a contractor marketing agency (2026).

Agencies often cost $3,500 to $8,000 a month. Here's what else is out there, and what you give up.

Updated December 2026 · 6 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

If an agency is out of budget, the main alternatives are: a done-for-you website subscription like ScrollBuilt ($249 a month billed yearly) or Hibu (listed from $449 a month); job software with a site included like Jobber (from $49 a month); an all-in-one toolkit like GoHighLevel ($97 a month and up) or Thryv; a review and messaging tool like Podium ($399 a month and up); or a do-it-yourself builder like Wix or Squarespace (from about $16 a month). What you give up is someone running paid ads for you.

What an agency gives you, and what it costs

An agency designs and writes your site, runs paid ads, handles SEO and reports back. Expect $3,500 to $8,000 a month, plus $12,000 to $25,000 to build a custom site, often on a 12-month contract. If you need ads managed and have the budget, it can be worth it.

What you give up without an agency

  • Paid ads management. Pair any option with an ads freelancer if you need it.
  • Strategy and reporting from a team.
  • Custom one-off design. Template-based options trade uniqueness for speed and price.

A common setup that works

A website subscription for the site and lead follow-up, plus an ads freelancer only if you run ads. You get the parts that win jobs without paying for a full retainer.

Questions

Common questions answered.

Do I need a marketing agency as a contractor?

Not necessarily. If referrals and search already bring you leads, a strong website and fast follow-up may matter more than ads. If you want ads run for you, an agency or freelancer helps.

What's cheaper than a marketing agency?

Every option on this page. A done-for-you website subscription costs a few hundred dollars a month; do-it-yourself builders cost less but take your time.
