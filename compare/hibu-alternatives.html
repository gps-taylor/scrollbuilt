Hibu alternatives for contractors (2026).

Why contractors look beyond Hibu, and the options worth a look.

Updated December 2026 · 6 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Contractors usually look for Hibu alternatives because of price (listed from $449 a month on Capterra) or the 12-month contract reviews report. The closest alternatives: ScrollBuilt for a contractor site with follow-up at $249 a month billed yearly; Thryv or GoHighLevel for an all-in-one toolkit; Jobber if you already run jobs there; Wix or Squarespace to do it yourself; Scorpion for full agency service.

Why contractors look for Hibu alternatives

  • Price. Hibu doesn't publish pricing; Capterra and Software Advice list its one plan, Hibu One, from $449 a month.
  • Commitment. Third-party reviews report a 12-month contract.
  • Fit. Hibu serves many kinds of local business; some contractors want a site built for their trade.

The alternatives

ScrollBuilt

$249 a month billed yearly, or $299 month to month. No setup fee.

Best for: Contractors who want a finished site built for their trade, plus lead follow-up.

Good: See your own draft in about 30 seconds before talking to anyone. Lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests built in.

Watch out for: Doesn't run paid ads. Read the comparison.

Thryv

Varies; a full system listed at $403 a month plus onboarding (November 2026).

Best for: Owners who want one system for scheduling, payments and marketing.

Good: Broad toolset in one place.

Watch out for: Some packages carry a six-month commitment. Read the comparison.

Jobber

Core from $49 a month, includes a basic website.

Best for: Owners who already run jobs in Jobber.

Good: Scheduling, quotes, invoicing and payments.

Watch out for: Basic template website. Read the comparison.

GoHighLevel

$97 to $497 a month plus usage.

Best for: Owners who like to build, or whose agency runs it.

Good: Very flexible toolkit.

Watch out for: Someone has to set it all up. Read the comparison.

Wix or Squarespace

From about $16 to $17 a month on yearly billing.

Best for: Owners with time to build it themselves.

Good: Cheap and flexible.

Watch out for: No lead follow-up; you do the work. Read the comparison.

Scorpion

Not published.

Best for: Larger operations wanting full agency service with ads.

Good: Websites, SEO, ads and video.

Watch out for: Typically 12-month contracts for marketing technology and SEO. Read the comparison.

Before you switch

  1. Check your current contract's end date and exit terms.
  2. Make sure your domain is registered in your name.
  3. Save your content and photos.
  4. Keep your old page addresses working with redirects so search results don't break.

Questions

Common questions answered.

What's the cheapest Hibu alternative?

Wix or Squarespace, at around $16 to $17 a month on yearly billing, if you build the site yourself. For a done-for-you contractor site, ScrollBuilt is $249 a month billed yearly.

Can I keep my domain if I leave Hibu?

If the domain is registered in your name, yes. Check who the registered owner is before you switch.
