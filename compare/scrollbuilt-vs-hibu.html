ScrollBuilt vs Hibu: which fits a contractor?

Both build your website for you. They differ on price, commitment and what the site is built to do.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Hibu builds and hosts a website with marketing add-ons; it doesn't publish prices, and third-party listings put its one plan at $449 a month, with reviews reporting a 12-month contract. ScrollBuilt is $249 a month billed yearly or $299 month to month, built for contractors, and lets you see your own draft before you talk to anyone.

Choose Hibu if

  • You want one company to handle your website plus listings, ads and other marketing.
  • You're comfortable with a longer commitment for a done-for-you service.
  • You don't need a site built specifically for your trade.

Choose ScrollBuilt if

  • You're a pool builder or landscaper and want a site that shows the work coming to life.
  • You want to see your own site before any sales call.
  • You want to pay less, or go month to month.
  • You want fast lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests built in.

Can you use both?

Yes, though most owners pick one website. If you like a Hibu marketing service such as listings, you can keep it and point your website to ScrollBuilt. Check your Hibu contract terms first.

Questions

Common questions answered.

How much does Hibu cost?

Hibu doesn't publish pricing on its site. Capterra and Software Advice list its one plan, Hibu One, from $449 a month as of 2026. Get a written quote from Hibu.

Does Hibu require a contract?

Third-party reviews report a 12-month contract. Confirm the term and early exit cost with Hibu before you sign.

Is ScrollBuilt a Hibu alternative?

Yes, for contractors who mainly want a strong website and lead follow-up. ScrollBuilt doesn't run paid ads, so if you want ads managed you'd pair it with an ads service.
