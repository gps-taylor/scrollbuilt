ScrollBuilt vs a Jobber website: which site should a contractor use?

Jobber runs your jobs. ScrollBuilt wins them. They're built for different halves of the business.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Jobber is field service software for scheduling, quotes and invoicing, and its Core plan, from $49 a month with no commitment, includes a basic website. ScrollBuilt is a website built to sell big projects, with a build film and lead follow-up, at $249 a month billed yearly or $299 month to month. Many contractors use Jobber for operations and a separate site to win the work.

Choose a Jobber website if

  • You need a simple site and already pay for Jobber.
  • Most of your work is recurring service where online booking matters most.
  • Budget is the main concern.

Choose ScrollBuilt if

  • You sell big projects, like pools or full backyards, where the site has to make people picture the result.
  • You want lead replies, reminders, proposal pages and review requests aimed at winning the job.
  • You want a site designed for your trade without building it yourself.

Can you use both?

Yes, and it's a common setup: ScrollBuilt for the website and lead follow-up, Jobber for scheduling, quotes and invoicing once the job is won. Leads arrive in your ScrollBuilt inbox, and you add the ones you win to Jobber.

Questions

Common questions answered.

Does Jobber include a website?

Yes. Jobber's Core plan, from $49 a month with no commitment as of late 2026, includes a basic website, and higher plans include it too.

Does ScrollBuilt replace Jobber?

No. ScrollBuilt doesn't do scheduling, invoicing or payments. It's the website and the follow-up that wins the job.

Is ScrollBuilt worth it if I already have a Jobber site?

If you sell large projects and your site doesn't show your work well, a site built to sell can win more of the leads you already get. See yours free first and compare.
