ScrollBuilt vs Scorpion: agency or website subscription?

Scorpion is a full-service marketing agency. ScrollBuilt is a website and follow-up subscription. They solve different-sized problems.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Scorpion is a large marketing agency for home services, law firms and healthcare, with websites, SEO and ad management; it doesn't publish prices, and its FAQ says it typically uses 12-month contracts for marketing technology and SEO. ScrollBuilt is $249 a month billed yearly or $299 month to month, focused on the website and lead follow-up, without paid ads.

Choose Scorpion if

  • You want an agency to run paid ads, SEO and video for you.
  • You have a meaningful monthly marketing budget.
  • You run a larger or multi-location operation and want dedicated reporting.

Choose ScrollBuilt if

  • You mainly need a website that wins homeowners over, plus fast follow-up.
  • You want a published price and the option to go month to month.
  • You want to see your own site in 30 seconds, before any meeting.
  • You're a pool builder or landscaper.

Can you use both?

Some contractors do: an agency or freelancer runs ads, and ScrollBuilt is the website and follow-up those ads send people to. Check that your agency is happy to send traffic to a site it didn't build.

Questions

Common questions answered.

How much does Scorpion cost?

Scorpion doesn't publish pricing. Its FAQ says the investment depends on your business goals. You'll get a number through its sales process.

Do you own your website with Scorpion?

Scorpion's FAQ says you own your website after the length of your contract is complete. Get the details in writing before you sign.

Does ScrollBuilt do SEO and ads like Scorpion?

ScrollBuilt sets up your site to be found and includes a "Get found for" plan and a monthly visibility report. It doesn't run paid ads.
