ScrollBuilt vs GoHighLevel: toolkit or finished site?

GoHighLevel is a powerful toolkit you assemble. ScrollBuilt is a finished contractor site you can see in 30 seconds.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

GoHighLevel (HighLevel) is all-in-one marketing software built mainly for agencies: CRM, funnels, websites, texting and automation, at $97, $297 or $497 a month plus usage fees for texts, calls, email and AI. You or an agency build it. ScrollBuilt is a finished contractor site with follow-up built in, at $249 a month billed yearly or $299 month to month.

Choose GoHighLevel if

  • You or someone on your team enjoys building funnels and automations.
  • You want maximum flexibility and are happy to set it up.
  • You already work with an agency that runs GoHighLevel for you.

Choose ScrollBuilt if

  • You want a finished site, not a project.
  • You want a design made for your trade, with a build film.
  • You want predictable pricing without usage fees for the core features.
  • You want to see your site before you commit.

Can you use both?

You could, but the two overlap a lot. Most contractors pick one: GoHighLevel if they want to build and tinker, ScrollBuilt if they want it done.

Questions

Common questions answered.

How much does GoHighLevel cost?

GoHighLevel's plans are $97, $297 and $497 a month as of late 2026, with usage-based charges for texts, phone calls, email and AI on top.

Is GoHighLevel good for contractors?

It can do a lot, but it's built mainly for agencies, and someone has to set it up. Contractors often use it through an agency that manages it.

Does ScrollBuilt have a CRM?

ScrollBuilt has a lead inbox with instant replies, reminders, proposal pages and review requests. It's built for winning jobs, not as a general-purpose CRM.
