ScrollBuilt vs Podium: messaging tool or website?

Podium handles messaging and reviews. ScrollBuilt is the website plus the follow-up, including review requests.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Podium is messaging, reviews and payments software for local businesses; it doesn't build your website. Third-party reviews of its pricing put Core at $399 a month and Pro at $599 a month, and buyers report 12-month agreements. ScrollBuilt is a contractor website with lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests at $249 a month billed yearly or $299 month to month.

Choose Podium if

  • You already have a website you're happy with.
  • Your main need is texting customers and collecting reviews at volume.
  • You have several locations and staff sharing one inbox.

Choose ScrollBuilt if

  • You need a website that wins homeowners over, not just a messaging tool.
  • You want lead replies, reminders and review requests included with the site.
  • You want to pay less, or go month to month.

Can you use both?

Yes, if you need Podium's messaging at volume. ScrollBuilt would be your website and lead follow-up, and Podium your texting and reviews hub. For many single-location contractors, ScrollBuilt's built-in review requests are enough on their own.

How much does Podium cost?

Third-party reviews of Podium's pricing list Core at $399 a month and Pro at $599 a month as of 2026, with add-ons such as phones extra. Confirm with Podium.

Does Podium build websites?

No. Podium focuses on messaging, reviews and payments. You'd need a separate website.

Does ScrollBuilt send review requests?

Yes. You can send a review request from your phone in a couple of taps when a job wraps up.
