Guide · Speed

Does website speed matter for contractors?

Yes. Homeowners look you up on their phones, often on cell signal. A slow site loses them before they see your work.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Speed matters because most visitors are on phones. Google has reported that 53% of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Test your site with Google's PageSpeed Insights, then fix oversized photos, heavy video backgrounds and extra plugins first.

Why speed matters

Google reported in 2016 that 53% of mobile visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Phones are faster now, but expectations have kept pace. A homeowner checking you out between errands won't wait.

How to test your site in one minute

  1. Go to Google's PageSpeed Insights and enter your web address.
  2. Look at the mobile score first. Above 90 is good; under 50 needs work.
  3. Then open your site on your phone with Wi-Fi off and count the seconds.

The usual causes

  • Huge photos. A 6 MB photo straight from a camera, shown at the size of a playing card.
  • Video backgrounds. Pretty, heavy and often silent anyway.
  • Too many plugins and widgets. Every chat bubble, slider and tracking script adds weight.
  • Cheap or overloaded hosting.
  • Old themes. Built before phones mattered.

What to fix first

  1. Resize and compress photos, and use modern formats like WebP.
  2. Remove plugins and widgets nobody uses.
  3. Replace a video background with one strong photo.
  4. If it's still slow, the theme or host is the problem, and patching gets expensive.

Questions

Common questions answered.

What's a good website speed score?

On Google's PageSpeed Insights, above 90 on mobile is good, 50 to 89 needs some work, and under 50 is slow.

Does speed affect Google rankings?

Page experience, including speed, is one of many things Google considers. The bigger effect is on people: slow sites lose visitors before they see your work.
