How to edit your website from your phone.

If updating your website needs a laptop and a free evening, it won't happen. Here's how to do it from the truck.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Use a website service with a phone-friendly editor, or one where you can ask for a change in plain words and approve a preview. Upload job photos straight from your phone the day you finish. Make one change at a time.

Your options

  • Do-it-yourself builder apps. Wix and Squarespace both have phone apps. Good for small text changes; fiddly for layout.
  • Text your web person. Works if they answer fast and don't bill by the quarter hour.
  • Ask in plain words. Some services let you type what you want changed and approve a preview before it goes live.

What's worth updating from your phone

  • Finished job photos. The day you finish, while the yard looks its best.
  • Services. Add a service you've started offering; remove one you've stopped.
  • Service area. New cities you're working in.
  • Seasonal message. Booking spring installs, fall cleanups, holiday hours.

Tips for job photos that sell

  1. Shoot wide, holding the phone sideways, standing where the homeowner would stand.
  2. Go back at dusk if there's lighting or water. It's the shot that sells.
  3. Clear tools, hoses and trash cans out of the frame.
  4. Caption it: city, scope, year.
  5. Get the homeowner's OK before posting photos of their home.

How it works on ScrollBuilt

In the ScrollBuilt portal, which works fully on a phone, you type what you want changed in plain words ("add pavers to my services", "swap the top photo"), see a preview and approve it. You can upload job photos from your phone. One change a month made by a person is included. Texting a change or a job photo to your site is coming once carrier approval is through.

Questions

Common questions answered.

Can I update my website without a computer?

Yes, with a phone-friendly editor or a service that takes change requests in plain words. Photos are the easiest thing to keep fresh from a phone.

How often should I update my website?

Add finished projects as you complete them, and check your services, area and seasonal message every month or two.
