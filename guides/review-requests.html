How contractors get more Google reviews.

Most happy customers will leave a review. They just need to be asked at the right moment, with a link that takes one tap.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Ask in person on the day you finish, then send a direct review link the same day. Follow up once. Reply to every review. Don't offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and never write or buy them.

Ask at the right moment

The best time is the final walkthrough, when the homeowner is standing in the finished yard. Ask in person first: "Would you mind leaving us a quick review? It really helps a small business like ours." Then send the link before you leave the driveway.

Make it one tap

In your Google Business Profile, find the option to ask for reviews and copy your direct review link. Send that link, not your website or a search page.

What to send

Thanks again for choosing [Company] for your [project], [Name]. If you have a minute, a quick review helps other homeowners find us: [link]. Mentioning your city and what we built helps most.

Asking them to mention the city and project is fair, and it makes the review more useful to the next homeowner.

Follow up once

If nothing comes in after three or four days, send one friendly reminder. After that, let it go.

Reply to every review

Thank people by name and mention the project. For a negative review, reply calmly, take the details offline, and fix what you can. Future customers read the replies.

The rules

  • Don't offer anything in exchange for a review. Google's policies prohibit incentives such as discounts or gifts for reviews.
  • Don't ask only happy customers to review on Google. Screening customers before sending them to Google isn't allowed under Google's policies.
  • Never write, buy or fake reviews. The FTC bans fake reviews, and Google removes them.
  • Text only customers who gave you their number for the job, and keep it about that job.

Where ScrollBuilt fits

ScrollBuilt lets you send a review request from your phone in a couple of taps when a job wraps up.

Questions

Common questions answered.

Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Google's policies prohibit offering incentives for reviews, and it can lead to reviews being removed.

How many reviews does a contractor need?

There's no magic number. Recent, steady and specific reviews matter more than a big total from years ago.

Should I reply to bad reviews?

Yes. A calm, specific reply shows future customers how you handle problems.
