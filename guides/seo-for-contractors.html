SEO for contractors: a plain-English guide.

Search engine optimization sounds technical. For a contractor, most of it is deciding what you want to be found for and saying it clearly.

Updated December 2026 · 7 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Pick the services and cities you want work from. Give each main service its own page. Complete your Google Business Profile. Earn reviews and mentions on other sites. Keep your site fast. Then give it months, not weeks. Nobody can promise rankings.

Step 1: Decide what to be found for

Write down the jobs you want more of and where. "Pool remodels in Henderson" is a target. "Pools" is a wish. Two or three services and five or six cities is plenty to start.

Step 2: Give each main service its own page

A page for pool remodels, one for new builds, one for spas. Each says what you do, where, what it costs roughly, how long it takes, and shows projects. One strong page per service beats one long page that lists everything.

Step 3: Set up Google Business Profile

Choose the right primary category, list your service area, add photos often, and keep hours and phone current. For local searches, this often shows before your website does.

Step 4: Earn reviews

Steady, specific reviews help you show up in local results and help homeowners choose you. See how to get more reviews.

Step 5: Get mentioned on other sites

Directories, your trade association, supplier dealer locators, your chamber of commerce, local news. Each is a vote that you're real and where you say you are.

Step 6: Keep the site fast and readable

Fast on phones, text in text (not baked into images), clear headings, captions on photos. See our speed guide.

Step 7: Give it time and check monthly

Set up Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. Once a month, look at which searches show your site and which pages get visits.

What to skip

  • Ranking guarantees. Nobody can promise rankings, and we won't.
  • Bought links. They can get a site penalized.
  • Dozens of near-identical city pages. Write real pages for real service areas, or don't.
  • Keyword stuffing. Write for the homeowner reading it.

And now, AI answers

The same basics help you show up when homeowners ask ChatGPT or Google's AI who to hire. Mentions on other sites carry extra weight there. See how contractors show up in ChatGPT.

Questions

Common questions answered.

How long does SEO take for a contractor?

Usually months. New pages can be indexed in days, but showing up well for competitive local searches takes steady work on pages, reviews and mentions.

Do I need to pay for an SEO company?

Not to get started. Steps 1 to 6 are things an owner can do. An SEO company makes sense when you're competing in a crowded market and have the budget.
