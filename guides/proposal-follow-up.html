Guide · Follow-up

How to follow up on a proposal without being pushy.

Most quotes don't get a no. They get silence. A steady, polite rhythm turns a lot of that silence into a yes or a clear no.

Updated December 2026 · 5 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Send the proposal within a day or two of the site visit and walk them through it if you can. Follow up when they've opened it, then around day 2, day 5 and day 10, and close it out politely at two weeks. Each message should be short and offer something useful.

Send it while they're still excited

A proposal that arrives a week after the site visit lands on a homeowner who has already met two other builders. Aim for within 48 hours.

Walk them through it

If you can, review the proposal on a short call or in person. You'll catch questions before they turn into silence.

Know when it's been opened

A proposal sent as a web page instead of a PDF can tell you when it's been opened. A call the day after they open it lands at the right moment.

A follow-up timeline

  1. Day it's opened: "Saw you had a chance to look. Any questions I can answer?"
  2. Day 2: a short note with one helpful thing: a similar project, your timeline, or financing details if you offer them through a lender.
  3. Day 5: "Wanted to check in. Is the scope right, or would it help to adjust anything?"
  4. Day 10: mention scheduling honestly: "We're booking [month] installs now."
  5. Day 14: "I'll close this out for now. If anything changes, reply here and I'll pick it back up."

When price comes up

Don't drop the price straight away. Ask what they're comparing against, and offer to adjust the scope instead: phase the project, change materials, simplify features. You keep your margin and they keep the project.

What not to do

  • Daily messages.
  • Fake deadlines or "today only" pricing.
  • Following up with nothing new to say.

Where ScrollBuilt fits

Questions

Common questions answered.

How long should I wait to follow up on a quote?

Follow up when they open it, or two days after sending if you can't tell. Then around day 5 and day 10, and close it out at about two weeks.

Is it pushy to follow up more than once?

Not if each message is short, polite and useful. Most homeowners appreciate a builder who stays organized.
