Marketing agency or website builder: which does a contractor need?

It comes down to budget, whether you need ads run for you, and how much you want to be tied in.

Updated December 2026 · 6 min read · By ScrollBuilt

In short

Hire an agency if you need paid ads managed and can spend $3,500 or more a month. Choose a done-for-you website subscription if you mainly need a strong site and fast lead follow-up at a few hundred dollars a month. Build it yourself if you have more time than budget.

When an agency makes sense

  • You want Google Ads, Local Services Ads or social ads run for you.
  • You have the budget for a monthly retainer and an ad spend on top.
  • You want a team doing strategy, writing and reporting.

Ask about the contract. Scorpion, one of the larger home services agencies, says in its FAQ that it typically uses 12-month contracts for marketing technology and SEO, with ads typically month to month, and that customers own their website once the contract is complete.

When a website subscription makes sense

  • Your phone rings from referrals and Google already, and you need a site that wins those people over.
  • You want leads answered fast without hiring office help.
  • You'd rather spend a few hundred dollars a month than a few thousand.

When doing it yourself makes sense

  • You enjoy it, or someone in the business does.
  • Your budget is tight and your time isn't.

The questions that matter more than price

  1. Who owns the domain? Register it in your name, whoever builds the site.
  2. Can I take the site with me? Some companies let you download everything; some make you rebuild.
  3. How long am I tied in? Get the contract length and early exit cost in writing.
  4. What happens to a lead? A pretty site that nobody answers from is a brochure.

You can mix them

Plenty of contractors use a website subscription for the site and follow-up, and a freelancer or agency for ads only. Ads send people to the site; the site and follow-up turn them into jobs.

Questions

Common questions answered.

Is an agency worth it for a small contractor?

It can be if you need ads run and have the budget. If your main need is a better website and faster follow-up, a subscription usually costs far less.

Can I leave ScrollBuilt and keep my site?

Your text, photos and leads are yours to download any time. On the annual plan the full working site can go with you from day one; on the monthly plan after three paid months, or earlier with a $999 buy-out.

Does ScrollBuilt run paid ads?

No. ScrollBuilt is the website and the follow-up behind it. Many owners pair it with an ads freelancer or agency.
