Pool builder questions

Good questions from pool builders.

More answers on the FAQ page. Last updated December 2026.

What is the best website builder for pool builders?

It depends on how much you want to do yourself. Do-it-yourself builders like Wix or Squarespace cost less but leave the design, writing and lead follow-up to you. Agencies do it all but often charge thousands a month. ScrollBuilt sits in between: a finished pool builder site in about 30 seconds, with lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests built in, from $249 a month billed yearly.

How much does a pool builder website cost?

Marketing agencies often charge $3,500 to $8,000 a month plus $12,000 to $25,000 to build. Do-it-yourself builders run about $17 to $159 a month, plus your time. ScrollBuilt is $249 a month billed yearly, or $299 month to month, with no setup fee. Your draft is free and you only pay when your site goes live.

How fast can I get a website for my pool company?

Your draft takes about 30 seconds: type your business name and city and it appears with a pool build film and your name on it. Going live is as fast as you add your photos and connect your web address. We can launch it for you for a one-time $499.

Do I need to know how to build a website?

No. The pages, design and build film already exist. You change things by asking in plain words and approving a preview, and one change a month made by a person is included.

Will my pool company show up in ChatGPT or Google’s AI answers?

Nobody can promise an AI will recommend a business, and we won’t. Your site is set up so search engines and AI assistants can find it, read it and understand what you build and where. A monthly report shows how you show up.

Is the build film my own pool project?

No. It comes from a library of sample films ScrollBuilt makes for pool builders, matched to your part of the country. Your own project photos go across the rest of the site, and you can switch films any time.

Can I take my website with me if I leave?

Your text, photos and leads are yours to download any time. On the annual plan, the full working site can go with you from day one. On the monthly plan, after three paid months, or earlier with a $999 buy-out.

Do you work with pool builders outside Las Vegas?

Yes. ScrollBuilt works with pool builders anywhere in the United States, and your draft picks a build film for your region.
