Pricing questions

Before you ask.

What is free?

Your draft. Type your business name and city and you get a real site with your name on it. No card and no call. Create a login to keep it and work on it for as long as you like.

What are the two prices?

It is one plan with two ways to pay. Annual is $249 a month billed yearly: $2,988 paid up front, which saves $600. Monthly is $299 a month. Both include everything in the plan.

What does the annual plan add?

A protected territory of up to five ZIP codes for the year, and the full working site to download from day one. On the monthly plan the full working site downloads after three paid months.

When do I start paying?

When you put your site live on your own web address. From then it is $299 a month by card, or $2,988 for the year on the annual plan. There is no setup fee.

Can I cancel?

On the monthly plan, any time: your plan stops at the end of the month you have paid for. The annual plan is paid for the year; the Terms of Service cover refunds.

What can I download, and when?

Your content is yours from day one: your text, photos, leads, redirects and search settings download any time, free. The full working site, with our design and the build film, downloads after three paid months, or from day one on the annual plan. You can also buy it out early for $999. Your web address stays registered in your name the whole time.

What is the $999 buy-out?

On the monthly plan, the full working site downloads after three paid months. If you want it sooner, a one-time $999 buys it out at any point. On the annual plan it is included from day one.

What counts as a small change?

One thing on one page that takes about 15 minutes: a new photo, new hours, a reworded paragraph, a price. A new page or a redesign is a bigger job and has its own fixed price.

Who makes changes to my site?

You can, by asking in plain words: type “change our hours to 7 to 5” and approve the preview. One change a month made by a person is included. Beyond that it is $49 a change, or $99 a month for Care, which covers unlimited small changes.

Is the pool visualizer included?

No. It is a $49 a month add-on for pool builders. A homeowner uploads a photo of their yard and sees a concept pool in it, and every request reaches you as a lead.

Can I switch build films?

Yes. Your draft picks a film for your trade and your part of the country. You can switch to any other film in the library at any time, at no charge.

What does a protected territory cover?

On the annual plan your territory is protected: we won’t build a site for another company in your trade located in your ZIP codes (up to five) for that year. It is about where a company is located. It does not stop a company based somewhere else from working in your area.

I have more than one location. How does that work?

One protected territory covers up to five ZIP codes around one location. A second location can add an extra protected territory, five more ZIP codes, at $99 a month, on the annual plan.

Do you build custom sites?

A custom site, designed around your business and run for you: custom pages, your real projects and reviews, extras such as a cost planner or a client portal, and changes handled for you. $1,500 setup and $599 a month on a 12-month term: $8,688 for the first year. Or from $7,500 to build, then $249 a month. Talk to us.
