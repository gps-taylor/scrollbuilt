Contractor questions

Good questions from contractors.

What is the best website builder for contractors?

It depends on who you want doing the work. Do-it-yourself builders cost less but leave design, writing and follow-up to you. Agencies do everything but often cost thousands a month. ScrollBuilt gives you a finished contractor site in about 30 seconds, with lead replies, reminders, proposals and review requests, from $249 a month billed yearly.

Which trades does ScrollBuilt work with?

Pool builders and landscapers can see an instant draft today. For custom home builders, remodelers, roofing and solar, ADUs and casitas, we build your draft with you by hand while templates for those trades are finished.

How much does ScrollBuilt cost?

$249 a month billed yearly, or $299 month to month, with no setup fee. Your draft is free and you only pay when your site goes live.

Do I need to know how to build a website?

No. The pages, design and build film already exist. You change things by asking in plain words and approving a preview, and one change a month made by a person is included.

Can I take my website with me if I leave?

Your text, photos and leads are yours to download any time. On the annual plan the full working site can go with you from day one; on the monthly plan after three paid months, or earlier with a $999 buy-out.

Will my business show up in Google and AI answers?

Nobody can promise rankings or that an AI will recommend a business, and we won't. Your site is set up so search engines and assistants can read it, with a plan for what to be found for and a monthly report on how you show up.
